How we think about land

We take on the projects other developers pass by.

Plenty of development companies want the easy site — flat, zoned, utilities at the curb. Those are also the sites everybody bids on. We'd rather find the awkward parcel near a major employment center that nobody has figured out yet, and do the work of figuring it out.

Being a general contractor first means we price a project from the ground up rather than from a spreadsheet. That's usually the difference between a deal that pencils and one that doesn't.

Two ways we work

Principal development and fee development.

As principal, we acquire rare, well-positioned land and value-add buildings near major employment centers and carry them through entitlement and construction ourselves.

As fee developer, we partner with owners and investors who have the land or the capital but need an experienced hand to take a project from concept to a class-A finished product — backed by an in-house brokerage that can help fill your building before it's even built.
