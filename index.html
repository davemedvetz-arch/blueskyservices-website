Crowd Funding

Commercial real estate investing, opened up.

Launched in 2016 under the JOBS Act, Blue Sky Crowd Funding gives everyday investors access to the same commercial real estate projects once reserved for institutions.

Ask About Investing
How it works

Two ways to put your capital to work.

Every offering is structured one of two ways, with full details spelled out in that project's offering documents.

  • Real estate loans (debt) — Invest in a loan secured by the property, earning interest with a balloon payment at maturity.
  • Equity investments — Pool funds with other investors to own a share of cash-flowing property, sharing in rents and eventual sale proceeds.
