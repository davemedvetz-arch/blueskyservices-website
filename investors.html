Why this exists

We're the developer, not a middleman.

Most real estate crowdfunding platforms are marketplaces — they list somebody else's deal and take a cut. We're not that. Blue Sky is the developer and the general contractor on the projects we offer, which means we've walked the site, priced the build, and have our own money in it.

That doesn't make an investment safe. Real estate never is. But it does mean the people describing the project to you are the same people responsible for delivering it.

Two structures

Two ways to put your capital to work.

Every offering is structured one of two ways, with the full details spelled out in that project's offering documents.

  • Real estate loans (debt) — Invest in a loan secured by the property, earning interest with a balloon payment at maturity.
  • Equity investments — Pool funds with other investors to own a share of cash-flowing property, sharing in rents and eventual sale proceeds.
